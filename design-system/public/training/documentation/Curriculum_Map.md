# Curriculum Map — ISO 20022 & SWIFT MX Practitioner Program

10-day instructor-led program. 5 exercises per day (50 total). Bilingual EN/AR throughout.

## Day 1 — ISO 20022 & CBPR+ Foundations
Learning outcomes: explain why MT/MX coexistence ended (22 Nov 2025); describe the 3-layer ISO 20022 message model; navigate MyStandards at a basic level.
Exercises: EX-D01-01 timeline reconstruction · EX-D01-02 MT-vs-MX family matching · EX-D01-03 MyStandards scavenger hunt · EX-D01-04 myth-busting quiz · EX-D01-05 discussion case.

## Day 2 — pacs.008 Customer Credit Transfer
Learning outcomes: read a full pacs.008 instance; map MT103 fields to pacs.008.
Exercises: EX-D02-01 annotate pacs.008 · EX-D02-02 MT103-to-pacs.008 mapping · EX-D02-03 spot errors · EX-D02-04 build pacs.008 from scenario · EX-D02-05 peer review.

## Day 3 — Cover Payments & pacs.009
Learning outcomes: serial vs cover method; construct pacs.009 COV; trace correspondent chain.
Exercises: EX-D03-01 decision tree · EX-D03-02 build pacs.009 COV · EX-D03-03 trace chain · EX-D03-04 find missing UETR · EX-D03-05 investigation case study.

## Day 4 — Structured Data & Addressing (SR2026)
Learning outcomes: apply structured address rules; handle structured remittance info.
Exercises: EX-D04-01 convert address · EX-D04-02 find non-compliant messages · EX-D04-03 structured remittance build · EX-D04-04 data-quality audit · EX-D04-05 client advisory memo.

## Day 5 — Reporting: camt.052/053/054
Learning outcomes: differentiate intraday, statement, notification messages; reconcile statement.
Exercises: EX-D05-01 camt.053 read-through · EX-D05-02 camt.054 vs MT900/910 · EX-D05-03 reconciliation · EX-D05-04 build camt.052 · EX-D05-05 discrepancy case.

## Day 6 — Exceptions: pacs.002, pacs.004, camt.029/056
Learning outcomes: interpret status reports/reason codes; process returns; handle investigations.
Exercises: EX-D06-01 reason-code drill · EX-D06-02 build pacs.004 · EX-D06-03 camt.056 walkthrough · EX-D06-04 camt.029 matching · EX-D06-05 role-play. Note: SR2026 pacs.002 reason-code detail is a tracked research gap.

## Day 7 — MyStandards & Validation
Learning outcomes: validate against usage guideline; interpret error reports.
Exercises: EX-D07-01 validation walkthrough · EX-D07-02 fix to pass · EX-D07-03 compare versions · EX-D07-04 build checklist · EX-D07-05 error triage.

## Day 8 — End-to-End Message Laboratory
Learning outcomes: process full payment lifecycle across linked MT/MX samples.
Exercises: EX-D08-01 lifecycle trace · EX-D08-02 inject-and-find errors · EX-D08-03 build full chain · EX-D08-04 SLA analysis · EX-D08-05 incident report.

## Day 9 — Operational Readiness & Client Context
Learning outcomes: build operational checklists; identify what needs client confirmation (ABK config never invented).
Exercises: EX-D09-01 readiness checklist · EX-D09-02 client FAQ draft · EX-D09-03 open questions list · EX-D09-04 self-assessment · EX-D09-05 peer teach-back.

## Day 10 — Capstone & Certification
Learning outcomes: demonstrate integrated competence under exam conditions.
Exercises: EX-D10-01 timed message-build · EX-D10-02 timed error-detection · EX-D10-03 reconciliation case · EX-D10-04 investigation case · EX-D10-05 final exam (~60 questions, randomized) then certificate on pass.
